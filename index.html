Click any place on the globe (or a pin). DRISHTI then does the whole job for that one spot, and shows every step while it works:

  1. Clouds: live hourly forecast for that exact spot
  2. Routes: flies every satellite's real orbit 48 h ahead and draws each pass over the spot
  3. Filter: strikes out passes that are too steep, in the dark, likely cloudy (neural net) or clash with the fleet plan; fuzzy logic ranks the rest
  4. Capture: the satellites fly the chosen passes and take the pictures
  5. Check: a fuzzy cloud mask scans every picture, pixel by pixel
  6. Deliver: the best picture is sent down at the next ground-station contact
